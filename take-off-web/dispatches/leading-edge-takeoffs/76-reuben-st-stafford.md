## What I did

Same workflow as Allambie:

1. **Retrieved** Drive bytes, decoded to `/tmp/takeoff/reuben/plan.pdf`.
2. **Rasterised at 75 DPI** to scan all 15 sheets, then 300 DPI on the wet-area sheet.
3. **Walked every sheet** — Cover · Site · GF · L1 · Elevations · Roof · Sections · Slab & Penetration · Wall Detail · Section Detail · **Landscape (pg 13)** · **Wet Area Details (pg 14)** · LHD Details (pg 15).
4. **Confirmed builder = Leading Edge** off the cover ("PROPOSED RESIDENCE FOR Leading Edge Constructions").
5. **Substrate per floor** — same as Allambie (and the Pavilion Studio template across LE jobs):
   - GF = concrete slab.
   - L1 = 19 mm Structa Floor on 400H joists (suspended timber — sheet-joint tape on L1 wet rooms).
6. **Identified 5 wet zones** off sheet 14: L1 **Ensuite** (off master), L1 **Bathroom** + adjoining **WC TILES** compartment, GF **Powder** (with 900×900 shower), GF **Laundry**.
7. **Measured** all internal dims off sheet 14 (figured dimensions).
8. **Priced** on LE simplified estimator.

### GP check ⚠
- Materials ~$1,149 (floor + walls + wastes per cost-data-memo §6)
- Labour: 3 days × 8 hr × $95/hr = $2,280
- Total cost ~$3,429
- **GP 38.5 %** — **1.5 points UNDER the 40 % floor**.

This is the second-smallest wet-floor area I've measured on an LE 2-storey (Reuben 23.28 m² vs Allambie 26.73 m²) and labour is fixed at 3 days regardless. The simplified rate barely clears costs at this floor area. Two responses:

1. **Quote as-is, take the lower GP.** Reuben sits inside the LE band ($6.1k vs band $6.6–7.8k), but at the cost of margin.
2. **Apply the 2026-05 proposed rate uplift** (rate cards §3 — floor lines bump 30–40 %). Re-pricing at proposed rates → ~$7.4k ex GST, ~50 % GP.
